About us

Second Chance Cars started with a simple idea: a used car should not come with a surprise. Every car on our lot is checked by a mechanic, test-driven, and priced fairly before we list it.

  • Inspected engine, brakes, tyres and electrics checked on every car.
  • Honest prices the price you see is the price you pay for that colour.
  • Real photos every picture is of the actual car on our lot.
  • After-sale help call us if anything worries you in the first weeks.
